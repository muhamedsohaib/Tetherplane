## Truth-in-licensing requirements

1. AGPL is an open-source license and allows commercial use subject to its conditions. Never say all commercial use requires paid permission.
2. Prior MIT/Apache versions may be commercially reused under those existing grants. New pricing must not purport to revoke those rights.
3. Dual licensing of future copyrightable additions requires that the licensor own or possess appropriate alternative-license grants for all relevant portions; a future CLA/assignment is a rights-process question, not automatic.
4. Do not promise exclusivity, warranties, releases, patent rights or trademark rights that are unavailable or uncleared.
